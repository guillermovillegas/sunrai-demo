# STORYBOARD.md — SunrAI, form C: the motion film

Status: delivered
Date: 2026-11-15
Owner: Guillermo Villegas
Renderer: HTML film page (plain CSS, virtual clock) + `playwright-core` frame stepping → ffmpeg x264
Master: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 76 s, silent with burned-in captions

**Form.** An editorial motion piece cut from the real product's own pixels. The
screens are the 2× captures in `videos/_capture/out/` — nothing is redrawn — but
they are treated as *material*: the camera pushes and tracks across them, masks
open on a region, figures are lifted out and held as type, and a connector draws
between two screens that belong to the same story. No cursor acts on a fake
surface; the only pointer is a pointing device over a real screen.

This is the form a trade-show loop can bear to repeat: it reads as a film, not as
a recording of someone's mouse.

## Logline

One contractor's account, read as a set of numbers and relations: thirteen jobs,
the phases inside one of them, the photos that prove the work, the hail that
creates the next thirteen.

## Brief

- **Audience:** first contact. Someone who has never seen SunrAI and has ninety
  seconds at a stand.
- **Where it plays:** the booth loop; the top of a landing page; a deck's title
  slide.
- **Must remember:** it is a field-to-office system of record — the photo, the
  phase, the invoice and the storm lead all live on the same job.
- **Constraints:** every frame's pixels are the real product. The typography and
  camera work are the film's own; the screens are not.

## Risks

- **Claims that need evidence.** "Thirteen jobs", "507 open tasks", "29
  overdue" and "5 inspections booked" were read from the seeded staging account
  with SQL against `projects`, `tasks` (status not `Done`) and `inspections`
  (status `scheduled`). They are written into the film, not typed from memory;
  re-deriving them means re-running the same three queries, and the numbers in
  `videos/README.md` are the record.
- **Assets that need a licence.** SunrAI's own landing photographs. Names and
  addresses are invented.
- **UI that must be the real product.** Every screen surface. Only the camera,
  the type and the connectors are the film's own.
- **Anything generated.** Nothing generated. No stock footage, no AI imagery.
- **Reading speed.** The last line is at its floor; that is recorded above rather
  than solved by making the type smaller.
